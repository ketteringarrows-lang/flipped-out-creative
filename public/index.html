EXPERIENCED HANDS.
FRESH THINKING.

I’m Wayne, the designer behind Flipped Out Creative. Based in Kettering, I help local businesses turn ideas into branding, print, signs and graphics that work in the real world.

I bring more than 30 years of graphic design experience, alongside decades of hands-on work installing graphics for events and exhibitions around the world.

That means I think about how a design will be printed, fitted and used as well as how it looks. From a business card to a shopfront or a touring activation, I can help carry the idea through to the finished job.

You’ll deal directly with me: someone who understands both the creative work and the practical details.
