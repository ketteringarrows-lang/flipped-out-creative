THE PROJECT

ONE IDENTITY.
ACROSS EVERY
FORMAT.

Accelerated Play brings together a range of social gaming activities, from bowling and shuffleboard to interactive darts. The design needed to give that varied offer a clear, recognisable identity.

We developed the brand identity and carried its visual language through an exhibition promotional video and a sales presentation, which was subsequently used as a printed brochure.

The result is a consistent family of materials that connects the brand introduction with the detail of the product range.

01 / BRAND IDENTITY

ENERGY, WITH
A CLEAR DIRECTION.

A distinctive AP mark, confident typography and a defined colour palette give the identity a consistent foundation across screen and print.

02 / PRESENTATION & BROCHURE

Image-led section openers introduce each activity. Structured product pages bring features and practical information together in a consistent layout, carrying the identity through the full sales story.
