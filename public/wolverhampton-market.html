THE PROJECT

TAKING THE DESIGN
ONTO THE SURFACE.

The market’s container graphics combine large food photographs, colour and branding across the corrugated exterior.

Our role covered vinyl production and installation, applying the supplied design to the containers. The work shown here is the finished application, with the graphics following the shape of the container surfaces.

IN ITS SETTING

A PART OF
THE MARKET.

The wider photograph puts the container structures in context, alongside the market entrance and public walkways.

The project demonstrates the production and installation side of our work: translating supplied artwork into finished vinyl graphics at scale.
