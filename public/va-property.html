THE PROJECT

A HIGH-STREET
SERVICE.
A DIFFERENT SETTING.

VA Property Consultants needed a pop-up property-agents office inside a shopping centre. The project brought the essentials of an estate agency into an open retail setting: property displays, a clear branded presence and somewhere for staff and customers to talk.

We handled the concept, design, production and installation, managing the project from the first idea through to the finished office.

The work brought spatial layout, display graphics and practical delivery together as one complete project.

DESIGN IN CONTEXT

VISIBLE FROM OUTSIDE.
WORKABLE INSIDE.

The branded panels face the shopping-centre walkways, with property listings above. Inside, desks, seating and screens create a working office within the pop-up’s footprint.
