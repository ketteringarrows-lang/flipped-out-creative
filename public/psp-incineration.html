THE PROJECT

THE BUSINESS.
ON THE VEHICLE.

The PSP support vehicle brings together the business name, its services and contact information in a red-and-blue livery.

We designed, produced and installed the vehicle graphics, carrying the artwork through to the finished van. Promotional print design forms part of the wider work for PSP, connecting the vehicle’s visual identity with the business’s other materials.
